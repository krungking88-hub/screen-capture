Screen Tools — Record · Screenshot · Snip

now share or record your experience

Screen Recorder

When you click Start Recording the browser asks which screen/window/tab to share. Choose audio if you want system/tab audio (browser dependent).

Screenshot & Snip

After taking a screenshot, draw a rectangle on the image to crop (snip) and then download it.
Privacy: nothing leaves your browser. No server involved.

Extras & Settings

If browser doesn't support encoder you'll get a default container.
  • Start Rec: click button (or call via code)
  • Stop Rec: click Stop
  • Screenshot: select screen when prompted & click Take Screenshot
Tip: prefer Chrome/Edge for best capture & audio support. Firefox may treat audio differently per platform.
